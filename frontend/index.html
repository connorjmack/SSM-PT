About this model
  • Currents: NOAA SSCOFS nowcast, hourly. "Surface" is the model's top layer, about the upper 1.6% of the water depth, not the skin layer.
  • Tracking: OceanTracker, 2-minute steps, currents interpolated between hours, plus random-walk diffusion.
  • Wind: only "Floating material" feels it, as a set share of the model's 10 m wind. That share also stands in for wave (Stokes) drift, which the model lacks.
  • Coast: water parcels and decaying substances bounce back off it. Floating material washes ashore and stays there, unless you untick "Washes ashore". The model's open-ocean edges act as coast for now.
  • Tidal flats: a particle the falling tide leaves on a dry flat is stranded until the water returns, then moves on.
  • Sinking particles and larvae need currents below the surface and are not available yet.
  • Arrows: the model's surface current at the nearest hour, one per patch of screen. Zoom in for detail in narrow passages.
  • Hourly model output under-resolves the strongest tidal currents.
  • Prototype: only a few hours of data are loaded.
